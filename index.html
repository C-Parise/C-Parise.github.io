Ropicks

Ropicks is a mining game developed in Unreal Engine 5 using Blueprints.

I created this project to test and apply the knowledge I acquired during my Game Engine 1 classes.

In the game, the player uses a pickaxe to mine rocks and collect resources. The player can upgrade the pickaxe to become stronger and mine larger rocks, allowing them to progress further through the game.

The project has been completed and was developed as a practical project to put my game development knowledge into practice.
